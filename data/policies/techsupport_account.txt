Account and Technical Support Policy

Users experiencing login failures should first be guided through the standard password reset flow. If error codes E-401 through E-410 persist after a password reset, this indicates a backend authentication service issue and should be escalated to Tier 2 Technical Support with the exact error code and timestamp.

Account lockouts after failed login attempts are automatically lifted after 30 minutes. Users may request an immediate unlock by verifying their identity via registered email or phone number.

For error code E-501 (payment processing conflict during account actions), the technical support team should coordinate with the Billing department, as this error is often linked to a billing dispute or duplicate charge on the account.

Data privacy: agents must never share full account details, including full payment card numbers, in written correspondence with customers.
