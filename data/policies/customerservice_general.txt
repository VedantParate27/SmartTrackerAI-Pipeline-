General Customer Service Policy

All complaints must be acknowledged within 24 hours of submission. High urgency complaints (as flagged by the triage system) must receive a substantive response within 4 business hours.

Agents are not authorized to promise refunds, compensation, or account credits beyond what is explicitly stated in the relevant department policy documents. Any exception requires supervisor approval.

Escalation path: if a customer's issue spans multiple departments (e.g., billing and technical support), the primary department is determined by the entity most central to the customer's core complaint, with a note left for the secondary department.

Customer tone: agents should remain professional and empathetic regardless of the customer's tone, and should never argue with a frustrated customer, even if the customer is factually incorrect about policy details.
