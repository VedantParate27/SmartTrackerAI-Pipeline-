Sanitary Waste Disposal Guidelines

Sanitary waste includes diapers, sanitary napkins, and similar personal hygiene products. This waste is treated as a biohazard risk and must be handled separately from both wet and dry waste.

Wrap sanitary waste securely in paper or a designated sanitary waste bag before disposal, to prevent leakage and reduce handling risk for waste collection workers.

Do not flush sanitary waste down toilets, as this causes blockages in sewage systems.

Dispose of sanitary waste in a clearly marked bin separate from recyclables, ideally collected on a specific schedule to minimize health risk to sanitation workers.
