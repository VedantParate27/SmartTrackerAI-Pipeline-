Wet Waste Disposal Guidelines

Wet waste includes food scraps, vegetable and fruit peels, garden waste, leaves, and cooked food waste. This waste is biodegradable and should be collected separately from dry waste.

Store wet waste in a green-colored bin or a clearly marked container. Empty this bin daily where possible, as wet waste decomposes quickly and can attract pests or produce odor within 24-48 hours.

Small quantities of wet waste from a household can be composted at home using a compost pit or a home composting unit, which reduces the amount sent to municipal collection and produces usable manure within 30-45 days.

Do not mix wet waste with plastic, glass, or other non-biodegradable materials, as this contaminates the composting process and makes municipal processing significantly harder.
