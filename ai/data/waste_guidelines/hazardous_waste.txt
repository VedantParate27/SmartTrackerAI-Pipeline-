Hazardous Waste Disposal Guidelines

Hazardous waste includes chemicals, paints, pesticides, batteries, and similar materials that can harm health or the environment if handled improperly.

Do not dispose of hazardous waste in regular wet or dry waste bins. Hazardous waste requires collection through designated hazardous waste drop-off points or scheduled special collection drives.

Store hazardous waste in its original container where possible, tightly sealed, and out of reach of children, until proper disposal is arranged.

Contact the local municipal body or environmental department to confirm the nearest hazardous waste collection point, as improper disposal can result in fines under local waste management regulations.
