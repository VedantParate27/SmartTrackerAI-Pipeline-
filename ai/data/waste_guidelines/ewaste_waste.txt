E-Waste Disposal Guidelines

E-waste includes electronics, batteries, wires, circuit boards, and household appliances. E-waste contains materials that are both valuable for recycling and potentially hazardous if discarded improperly.

Do not dispose of e-waste in regular household bins. E-waste must be handed over to authorized e-waste collection centers or recyclers.

Many electronics retailers and manufacturers offer take-back programs for old devices; check with the point of purchase before disposal.

Remove personal data from devices such as phones and computers before handing them over for recycling, as a data protection precaution.
