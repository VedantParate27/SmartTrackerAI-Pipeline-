Dry Waste Disposal Guidelines

Dry waste includes paper, cardboard, plastic, glass, metal, and rubber. This waste is largely recyclable and should be kept clean and dry.

Store dry waste in a blue-colored bin or a clearly marked container, separate from wet waste. Rinse containers that held food or liquid before discarding them, since contamination reduces recyclability.

Flatten cardboard boxes and plastic bottles where possible to save space and make collection more efficient.

Segregate dry waste further where local facilities allow: paper and cardboard in one group, plastics in another, and glass/metal in a third, since different recycling processes apply to each.
