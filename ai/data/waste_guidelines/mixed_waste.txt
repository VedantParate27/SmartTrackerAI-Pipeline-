Mixed Waste Disposal Guidelines

Mixed waste refers to a combination of wet, dry, and other waste categories deposited together without proper segregation. This is common in overflowing public bins or areas where segregation practices are not followed.

If you encounter mixed waste in a personal or household context, sort it into wet, dry, and hazardous categories as far as possible using the guidelines for each type, rather than disposing of it as a single mixed batch.

Mixed waste in public bins or community areas that has not been segregated at the source is a municipal collection and enforcement matter. Report the location so waste management staff can arrange proper collection and enforce segregation rules for that area.

Repeated mixed-waste accumulation at the same public location may indicate insufficient bin capacity or a lack of segregation enforcement, and should be flagged to the local municipal body for a longer-term fix rather than treated as a one-off cleanup.
