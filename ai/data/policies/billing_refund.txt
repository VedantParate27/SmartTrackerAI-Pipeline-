Refund and Billing Policy

Customers are eligible for a full refund within 30 days of purchase if the product is unused and in original packaging. Digital subscriptions may be refunded within 14 days of the billing date if the service was not used during that period.

Duplicate charges: if a customer is charged twice for the same transaction, the duplicate charge will be automatically reversed within 3-5 business days once verified by the billing team. Customers should provide the transaction ID or approximate charge date to expedite verification.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method only. Bank processing times may add an additional 3-7 business days beyond the point at which Company issues the refund.

Subscription cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle; no partial-month refunds are issued for early cancellation.
