Delivery Policy

Standard delivery timelines are 5-7 business days for domestic orders and 10-14 business days for international orders. If an order has not been delivered within 14 business days from the date of shipment confirmation, the customer is entitled to a full refund or a free replacement, at the customer's choice.

For orders delayed beyond 21 days with no tracking movement for over 7 consecutive days, the case must be escalated to the Logistics Manager for manual investigation, as this may indicate a lost shipment.

Customers who report non-delivery must provide their order ID. Refunds for non-delivery are processed within 5-7 business days after approval and are returned to the original payment method.

International orders delayed due to customs holds are not eligible for automatic refund but are eligible for a shipping credit of 10% of order value if the delay exceeds 30 days.
